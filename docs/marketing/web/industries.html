HOW TO READ THESE PAGES

Same platform, different floor.

Each industry page is a lens, not a different product. The five capability pillars — connectivity & edge, data acquisition, asset intelligence, condition monitoring, and operational intelligence — are the same everywhere. What an industry page does is foreground the pillars that matter most for that floor, map them to the solutions that fit, and answer the questions that industry asks first.

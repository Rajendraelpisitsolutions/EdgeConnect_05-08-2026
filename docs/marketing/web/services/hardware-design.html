Hardware Design & Development

Industrial electronics from concept to prototype and production support.

Elpis supports industrial and defense hardware development across schematic design, PCB layout, prototyping, sensor and interface integration, board bring-up, and hardware/software integration.

What we do

  • Schematic design and PCB layout
  • Prototyping and board bring-up
  • Sensor and interface integration
  • Hardware/software integration
  • Design for rugged, field-deployable electronics
  • ASIC-adjacent and ASIC-support activities, where the project scope and capability are separately confirmed

Where it applies

Industrial monitoring and control electronics, edge and gateway hardware, sensor systems, and defense electronics.

Scope & compliance

Certification and compliance scope (including CE, UL, ATEX, IP-rating, military-grade, aerospace-grade, and production-volume readiness) is defined project by project. Elpis does not claim these unless explicitly documented for the project.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 June 2026 · Elpis IT Solutions Pvt Ltd

This Privacy Policy describes how Elpis IT Solutions Pvt Ltd handles information collected through this website. It is provided in good faith; please review it with your own legal counsel before relying on it for compliance.

1. Who we are

Elpis IT Solutions Pvt Ltd (“Elpis”, “we”, “us”) operates the website at www.elpisitsolutions.com and provides industrial edge-connectivity and operational-intelligence products and services. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you contact us, request a resource, or fill in a form, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, work email, company, role, and any message content.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we collect basic technical and usage data (such as pages visited, approximate location derived from IP, browser type, and referring page) through cookies and similar technologies, to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

3. How we use your information

We use the information to respond to your enquiries, provide the materials or information you request, operate and improve the website, and — where you have asked to hear from us — to send relevant updates. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to measure site usage. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not function as intended if cookies are disabled.

5. Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who process it on our behalf (for example, hosting and analytics providers) under appropriate confidentiality obligations, and where required by law. We do not share your information for third-party advertising.

6. Data retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law, and we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. International offices

Elpis operates from offices in Bengaluru and Pune (India) and Matthews, North Carolina (USA). Information you provide may be processed at any of these locations under this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the current version.

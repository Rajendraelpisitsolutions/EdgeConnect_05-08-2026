# Customer outbound kit

Companion to the conversion layer (`/contact`, `/assessment`) and the
LinkedIn content plan. Goal: bring **customers** (not just vendors).
All asks route to **www.elpisitsolutions.com/assessment**.

---

## 1. Microsoft reply (co-sell, not "buy Fabric")

The Microsoft email is an automated Fabric pitch. Don't evaluate Fabric for
your own stack — redirect to the **partner / co-sell** motion, which is where
customer leads come from.

> Hi Riley,
>
> Thanks for reaching out. Elpis IT Solutions is an industrial systems integrator and product company — an Authorised AVEVA Member System Integrator, and the maker of edge-connectivity and operational-intelligence products (EdgeConnect, EREMOS V2) for manufacturers across India and the Middle East.
>
> Rather than evaluating Fabric for our own stack right now, what would be more useful is the partner side:
> 1. Co-sell / partner referrals — how Microsoft connects partners like us with mutual manufacturing customers.
> 2. Whether Fabric / Power BI is a fit as a delivery option for our data-analytics services clients.
> 3. Listing as an industrial SI in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program / marketplace.
>
> Could you connect me with the right partner / channel contact (rather than a Fabric sales motion)? Happy to share what we do — www.elpisitsolutions.com
>
> Best, Sudhakar — Elpis IT Solutions

*Why:* keeps it honest (no Fabric expertise claimed), and steers toward the
ecosystem that routes **customers** to partners.

## 2. AVEVA partner-directory checklist

The AVEVA credential is a customer magnet (it's what triggered the Microsoft
email). Make sure customers searching for an AVEVA integrator can find you.

- [ ] **Find your listing** — search AVEVA's public Partner Finder / SI locator (aveva.com → Partners) for "Elpis" and by region (India). Confirm the Member SI profile is **published and discoverable**.
- [ ] **If not listed / incomplete** — contact your AVEVA partner manager to publish/complete the profile.
- [ ] **Complete the profile:** company description, **regions served** (India, Middle East, USA), **AVEVA products** (System Platform, Plant SCADA, Historian), industries served, website + contact.
- [ ] **Ask the AVEVA partner team about:**
  - Lead referrals / deal registration — how customer enquiries get routed to Member SIs.
  - Co-sell and any partner marketing support.
  - The path from **Member** to a higher tier (often unlocks more leads) — training/certs required. *(Keep the public site at the exact current tier until upgraded.)*
- [ ] **Link it back** — once your AVEVA partner-finder URL is live, you can reference it from the `/services/aveva-scada-integration` page as third-party verification.

## 3. LinkedIn customer outbound

**Target titles:** Plant Manager, Production/Operations Director, Head of Manufacturing, OT/Automation Lead, Maintenance/Reliability Manager, Digital/Industry-4.0 lead.
**Target firms:** manufacturers (automotive, pharma, heavy manufacturing, defense suppliers) in **India + the Middle East**.
**Cadence:** small, personalised batches (20–30/week), not bulk. LinkedIn limits + quality matters.

### A. Connection request (note < 300 chars)
> Hi [Name] — I work with manufacturers connecting mixed-vendor machines (FANUC, Siemens, Brother, Modbus, OPC UA) to OEE and reporting, and we're an authorised AVEVA SCADA integrator. Keen to connect with operations leaders in [industry/region].

### B. First message (after they accept — value, soft ask)
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. We help plants get data off the controllers they already run — FANUC, Siemens, Brother, Modbus, OPC UA — into OEE, alarms, and reports, **beside** their existing SCADA/MES (no rip-and-replace). We also deliver AVEVA SCADA projects.
>
> If it's ever useful, we offer a **free, no-obligation assessment**: we look at your controller mix, what's realistically reachable today, and a practical approach. No pressure → www.elpisitsolutions.com/assessment
>
> Out of interest — how are you collecting machine data today?

### C. Follow-up (a week later — content, no pitch)
> Hi [Name], thought this might be relevant — a short, non-hype piece on why home-grown FANUC data collection gets fragile, and a maintainable alternative: www.elpisitsolutions.com/blog/fanuc-cnc-data-collection
>
> Are you collecting machine data today, or still mostly on manual logs?

### Tips
- **Personalise line 1** (their plant, role, a recent post). Generic = ignored.
- **Lead with their problem**, not your product. The assessment is the ask, not a demo.
- **Don't pitch in the connection note.** Connect first, value second.
- **Reply fast** when someone engages; move to a call.
- **Pair with the Tuesday blog drip** — outbound + content compound.
